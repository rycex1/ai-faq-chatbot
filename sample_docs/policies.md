# Store Policies

## Payment

We accept cash, all major credit and debit cards, and Apple Pay and Google Pay. We do not accept personal cheques.

## Cancellations and refunds

- Catering and cake orders can be cancelled for a full refund up to 72 hours before pickup.
- Cancellations made within 72 hours of pickup are refunded at 50%, because ingredients have already been bought.
- Custom-decorated cakes cannot be refunded once decorating has started, usually 2 days before pickup.
- If something you bought in the shop is not right, bring it back the same day with your receipt and we will replace it or refund you.

## Allergies

Our kitchen handles wheat (gluten), milk, eggs, tree nuts, peanuts, soy and sesame. We label every item that contains a major allergen, but because everything is made in one kitchen we cannot guarantee any item is free of traces of allergens. Please tell staff about allergies before you order.

## Pets

Dogs are welcome on the outdoor patio. Only service animals are allowed inside.

## Gift cards

Gift cards are sold in the shop in any amount from $10. They never expire and can be used for catering orders.
