# Hours and Location

Sunrise Bakery is a fictional neighbourhood bakery used as sample data for this project.

## Opening hours

- Monday: closed
- Tuesday to Friday: 7:00 am to 6:00 pm
- Saturday: 7:00 am to 4:00 pm
- Sunday: 8:00 am to 2:00 pm

Bread usually sells out by early afternoon on Saturdays. Call ahead if you want a specific loaf late in the day.

## Holiday hours

We close on New Year's Day, Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day. On Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve we close at 2:00 pm. Holiday pre-orders open on November 1 each year.

## Address and parking

Sunrise Bakery, 418 Maple Street, Riverton. We are on the corner of Maple and 5th, next to the public library. Free street parking is available on 5th Street, and there is a bike rack by the front door.
