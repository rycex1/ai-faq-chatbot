# Catering and Custom Orders

## What we cater

We supply pastry platters for office breakfasts, sandwich-loaf and bread boxes, and whole and tiered cakes for birthdays and small weddings. We do not provide staff, plates or delivery for events larger than 80 people.

## Platters

- Breakfast pastry platter (24 mini pastries): $65
- Croissant and fruit platter (serves 12): $80
- Cookie platter (30 cookies): $55

## How to order

Send your order to hello@sunrisebakery.example or call (555) 555-0142. Please include the date, pickup or delivery time, number of guests and any allergies.

- Platters need at least 48 hours' notice.
- Whole cakes need 48 hours' notice. Custom-decorated and tiered cakes need 7 days' notice.
- Orders over $200 require a 50% deposit when you place the order.

## Delivery

We deliver catering orders within 5 miles of the bakery for a flat fee of $15. Orders over $250 are delivered free. Delivery windows start at 7:30 am, Tuesday to Saturday. We do not deliver on Sundays or Mondays.
