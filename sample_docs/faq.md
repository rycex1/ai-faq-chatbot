# Frequently Asked Questions

## Do you have Wi-Fi and seating?

Yes. We have 20 seats inside and 12 on the patio, and free Wi-Fi. Laptops are welcome except on weekends from 9 am to 12 pm, when we keep tables free for people eating.

## Can I reserve a table?

We don't take table reservations. Groups of more than 8 can call ahead and we will do our best to set aside space.

## Do you sell day-old bread?

Yes. Bread from the previous day is sold at half price from a basket by the till until it runs out, usually by 10 am.

## Do you offer a loyalty program?

Yes. Ask for a stamp card: every 10th coffee is free. Stamp cards are not valid on catering orders.

## Are you hiring?

We post open jobs on the noticeboard in the shop. You can also email a short note to hello@sunrisebakery.example.

## Do you offer baking classes?

We run a sourdough basics class on the first Saturday of each month from 2:30 pm to 5:00 pm. It costs $75 per person, including a starter to take home, and has 8 places. Book by email or in the shop.
